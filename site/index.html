Also in the app

  • Home screen widget on Android: today’s Hukamnama, with buttons that play it and live kirtan without opening the app.
  • In the car: Android Auto plays live kirtan, the Hukamnama and Katha.
  • Siri on iPhone: “Hey Siri, start Kirtan in Live Darbar.”
  • Shortcuts: press and hold the app icon to start kirtan, the Hukamnama or Katha.
