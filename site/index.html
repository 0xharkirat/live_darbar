The app

Why Live Darbar

What it does well, shown in the app itself.

  • One tap, and you are there.

    Press play. Live kirtan from Sri Harmandir Sahib starts at once.

  • Today, on the first screen.

    The Nanakshahi date and the Gregorian date are the first thing you see.

  • The Hukamnama, line by line.

    Today's Hukamnama from SGPC as text, with SGPC's meaning in Punjabi or English. Make the text bigger. Listen to the recording. The Katha is there too.

  • Weak signal? Pick Data saver.

    Live kirtan at 28 kbps, about 13 MB an hour. Automatic mode drops to it when your signal is weak.

  • Live

    • A call
    • A headphone pause
    • A network drop

    It keeps playing.

    Through calls, headphone pauses and network drops. A recording resumes where it stopped.

  • Made for Amritvela.

    The whole app in Punjabi or English. Large type. A dark screen for Amritvela and night.

  • Free
  • No ads
  • No account
  • No tracking

The Google Play data safety form says: no data collected.
