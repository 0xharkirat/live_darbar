# Live Darbar — OACP Context

## What this app does
Live Darbar streams live Gurbani Kirtan from Sri Darbar Sahib (Golden Temple), Amritsar. It also plays the daily Mukhwak (Hukumnama) audio, the Mukhwak Katha (discourse/explanation), and displays the Hukumnama as a PDF document.

## Vocabulary
- **Kirtan**: Sikh devotional singing/music
- **Gurbani**: Sacred hymns from Guru Granth Sahib
- **Mukhwak / Hukumnama**: The daily divine order read from Guru Granth Sahib at Darbar Sahib
- **Katha**: Discourse or explanation of the Hukumnama
- **Darbar Sahib**: The Golden Temple in Amritsar, Punjab
- **SGPC**: Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee (manages Sikh shrines)

## Capabilities
- play_live_kirtan: Starts the live kirtan audio stream from Darbar Sahib
- play_mukhwak: Plays today's Hukumnama audio recording
- play_katha: Plays today's Katha (discourse on the Hukumnama)
- view_mukhwak_pdf: Opens the daily Hukumnama PDF for reading

## Disambiguation
- "play kirtan" / "start gurbani" / "golden temple live" → play_live_kirtan
- "play mukhwak" / "hukumnama audio" / "today's hukum" → play_mukhwak
- "play katha" / "hukumnama explanation" / "discourse" → play_katha
- "show mukhwak" / "read hukumnama" / "view hukumnama pdf" → view_mukhwak_pdf
- Key distinction: "play/listen" = audio actions, "show/read/view" = view_mukhwak_pdf

## Phonetic variations (for voice recognition)
- Mukhwak: muk wak, mukh wak, muk walk, mukh walk, muck walk, mock walk, wak, walk
- Hukumnama: hukum, hukam, hukum nama
- Katha: kaatha, kathaa
- Kirtan: keertan, keerthan
